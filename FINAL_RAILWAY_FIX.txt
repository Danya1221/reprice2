This build fixes it without requiring a Railway Settings change.

    buildCommand = chmod 755 start.sh
    startCommand = ./start.sh

Procfile fallback:
    web: bash start.sh

So even if GitHub/browser upload strips the executable flag,
Railway restores it during build before deployment.
